# Catalog

The catalog maps operator use cases to OTel model slots and platform blueprints. Four flagship blueprints arrive in Phase 5, and the full use-case catalog arrives in Phase 6.

Planned domains:

- Knowledge assistance
- Closed-loop incident handling
- Alarm and root-cause triage
- Customer-care diagnostics
- Churn
- Revenue assurance
- Energy
- Slice service-level agreements (SLAs)
- Method of procedure (MOP) validation
- Transport and IETF diagnostics
- Cross-domain OSS correlation
