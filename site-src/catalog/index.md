# Catalog

The catalog maps operator use cases to OTel model slots and platform blueprints. Four flagship use-case blueprints come first, then the full catalog. Each catalog entry links to its use-case blueprint and to the platform blueprints it depends on.

| Blueprint | Topic | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| [UC-KNOW-01](standards-knowledge/grounded-knowledge-assistant/README.md) | Grounded knowledge assistant | Standards knowledge |
| [UC-AGOPS-01](agentic-operations/closed-loop-incident-handling/README.md) | Governed closed-loop incident handling | Agentic operations |
| [UC-OPS-01](network-operations/alarm-triage-adaptation/README.md) | Adaptation ladder on alarm and root-cause triage | Network operations |

Planned domains:

- Knowledge assistance
- Closed-loop incident handling
- Alarm and root-cause triage
- Customer-care diagnostics
- Churn
- Revenue assurance
- Energy
- Slice service-level agreements (SLAs)
- Method of procedure (MOP) validation
- Transport and IETF diagnostics
- Cross-domain OSS correlation
